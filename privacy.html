腕语(WatchChatBot)隐私政策

最后更新:2026-10-02(适用于 3.0 及以后版本)

腕语(下称"本 App")没有账号系统,不追踪你,没有任何广告或分析 SDK。本 App 有三种用 AI 的方式,数据去向不同,下面分别说清楚。

1. 三种用法,数据去哪

① 腕语云(免费,所有用户可用):你问的文字、照片,以及开启"自研语音输入"时的录音,会经开发者运营的网关(Cloudflare Workers,sotto-cloud.tusu-jason.workers.dev)转发给 硅基流动(SiliconFlow) 生成回答或转成文字。第一次使用前,App 会说明这一点并征得你的明确同意;你可以随时在"默认模型 → 管理模型条目 → 腕语云 → ⓘ"撤回,撤回后不再发出任何内容。硅基流动如何处理你的数据,受其隐私政策约束。

② 自己的 key(BYOK):本 App 用你自己配置的密钥,把提问直接发给你自己选择的 AI 服务商(如通义千问、Anthropic,或任意 OpenAI 兼容服务)。这些请求不经过我们,受该服务商的隐私政策约束。开启"自研语音输入"时,录音同样直接发给你选的转写服务。

③ Apple 智能(iOS 27 / watchOS 27 起,支持的设备):提问由 Apple 的私有云计算(Private Cloud Compute)处理,不经过我们,Apple 不保存你的内容,受 Apple 隐私政策约束。

没有开启"自研语音输入"时,语音由 Apple 系统听写转成文字(由 Apple 处理,受 Apple 隐私政策约束)。

2. 腕语云网关保留什么

  • 不保存任何内容:文字、照片、录音只做转发,不落盘、不进日志。
  • 匿名设备标识:App 在你设备上随机生成一个标识(只存本机钥匙串,不同步、不随备份迁移),随请求发出,用于按设备计算免费额度。网关只保存它的哈希值。手表与 iPhone 各算一台设备。
  • 防刷核验:为防止反复重装或换设备刷取免费额度，App 会尝试向网关提供由 Apple 签发的 App Store 下载凭证和设备核验凭证。凭证是否可用，取决于设备、安装渠道及服务配置。网关对有效下载凭证验签后，只保留账户下载标识的单向哈希；在设备核验服务启用且可用时，还会向 Apple 查询和设置本 App 的设备登记状态。无法核验时按未核验设备额度处理，不授予已核验新用户福利。我们不会获得你的 Apple ID、姓名、邮箱或电话。
  • 用量计数(按设备 / 账户哈希):每日次数 / token 数 / 转写秒数保留 7 天;设备登记与设备 → 账户哈希的绑定 90 天未使用即删除;账户哈希(首见日期、是否领过首日福利)400 天未使用即删除;不含任何哈希的全站每日汇总保留 400 天。
  • 运行日志只有计数、状态码、国家 / 地区代码、设备哈希前 8 位;没有内容、没有 IP 地址、没有完整标识。

3. 数据存在你设备上的

  • API 密钥(BYOK):API 密钥保存在 iPhone 与 Apple Watch 的系统钥匙串中，经 Apple 的加密设备间通道同步。调用 BYOK 服务时，密钥作为鉴权信息直接发给你选择的服务商；不会经过腕语云，不写入本 App 的文件或诊断日志。服务商如何处理鉴权信息，以其政策为准。
  • 对话记录与设置:只存于你设备本地,可随时删除。
  • 诊断日志:只记录事件名与状态码,不含对话内容、不含密钥,只存本地;仅当你主动使用"反馈问题"并选择附带时才随你亲手发送的邮件带出。

4. 权限

  • 麦克风:仅在你开启"自研语音输入"并主动按下录音时使用。不开启则不采集音频。

5. 儿童

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童,也不刻意收集其信息。

Sotto (WatchChatBot) Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-02 (applies to version 3.0 and later)

Sotto has no accounts, does not track you, and contains no ads or analytics SDKs. There are three ways to use AI in the app, and your data goes to different places in each.

1. Three ways, and where your data goes

① Sotto Cloud (free, available to everyone): the text and photos you ask about — and your recordings if you turn on custom voice input — are relayed through a gateway run by the developer (Cloudflare Workers, sotto-cloud.tusu-jason.workers.dev) to SiliconFlow, which generates the answer or transcript. Before your first use, the app explains this and asks for your explicit consent; you can withdraw it anytime under "Default model → Manage model entries → Sotto Cloud → ⓘ", after which nothing more is sent. SiliconFlow's handling of your data is governed by its own privacy policy.

② Your own key (BYOK): the app uses your own key to send prompts directly to the AI provider you choose. These requests do not pass through us and are governed by that provider's policy. With custom voice input on, recordings also go directly to the transcription service you choose.

③ Apple Intelligence (iOS 27 / watchOS 27 on supported devices): your prompts are processed by Apple's Private Cloud Compute — not through us; Apple doesn't keep your content, under Apple's privacy policy.

With custom voice input off, speech is transcribed by Apple's system dictation (handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy).

2. What the Sotto Cloud gateway keeps

  • No content: text, photos and audio are relayed only — never stored or logged.
  • Anonymous device identifier: a random identifier generated on your device (kept in this device's Keychain only, not synced or migrated), sent with requests to apply per-device free quotas. The gateway stores only its hash. Your watch and iPhone count as two devices.
  • Abuse protection: To discourage repeated reinstalls or device changes to obtain extra free usage, the app attempts to provide the gateway with Apple-issued download and device-verification credentials. Availability depends on the device, distribution channel and service configuration. After verifying a valid download record, the gateway keeps only a one-way hash of its account download identifier. When device verification is enabled and available, the gateway also queries and updates this app’s device-registration state with Apple. Unverified devices receive the unverified allowance and do not receive the verified new-user bonus. We do not receive your Apple ID, name, email or phone number.
  • Usage counters (by device / account hash): daily requests / tokens / transcription seconds are kept for 7 days; device registration and device → account-hash bindings are deleted after 90 days without use; account hashes (first-seen date, whether the first-day bonus was used) are deleted after 400 days without use; daily site-wide totals with no hashes are kept for 400 days.
  • Operational logs contain only counts, status codes, country/region codes and the first 8 characters of a device hash — no content, no IP addresses, no full identifiers.

3. What stays on your devices

  • API keys (BYOK): API keys are stored in the system Keychain on your iPhone and Apple Watch and synced over Apple’s encrypted device-to-device channel. When you use a BYOK provider, the key is sent directly to that provider for authentication. It does not pass through Sotto Cloud and is not written to this app’s files or diagnostic logs. The provider’s handling of authentication data is governed by its policy.
  • Conversations and settings: stored only on your device; delete them anytime.
  • Diagnostic logs: event names and status codes only (never conversation content or keys), stored locally; they leave your device only if you attach them to feedback email you send yourself.

4. Permissions

  • Microphone: used only when you turn on custom voice input and actively record. Otherwise no audio is captured.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.
